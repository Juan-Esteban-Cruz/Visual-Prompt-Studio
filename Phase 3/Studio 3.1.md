Create an original oil painting representing an idealized Renaissance equestrian portrait set in the Italian countryside during the High Renaissance. Depict a nobleman wearing historically inspired Renaissance clothing, riding a majestic horse through a peaceful landscape surrounded by ancient Roman ruins, marble columns, olive trees, and distant mountains.

Incorporate the classical architecture, balanced composition, perspective, and humanist ideals associated with Raphael's The School of Athens, without reproducing either original composition.

The composition should also reflect the Renaissance revival of classical Greek and Roman culture, humanism, and admiration for ancient civilization.

Include varied textures in the horse's coat, the rider's clothing, the marble architecture, and the natural landscape.
Pay particular attention to value and shading: use gradual transitions between light and dark tones, soft shadows, highlights from natural sunlight, and atmospheric perspective to create depth. Use a balanced distribution of visual elements, with the rider and horse as the main focal point.
The final artwork should demonstrate the artistic principles of the High Renaissance: harmony, proportion, naturalism, perspective, balance, and classical beauty.

The final artwork should communicate how ancient Mesopotamian civilizations used visual imagery, symbolic animals, decorative architecture, and representations of social hierarchy to express political power, religious beliefs, protection, and stability.
